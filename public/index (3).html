How to play

Hit the target using the four numbers and + − × ÷. You get six tries, and there's a new puzzle every day.

  • Use all four numbers, each one exactly once per try.
  • Use brackets to control the order of operations.
  • Division has to come out to a whole number at every step.
  • Every step must stay positive: no zero or negative results.

Example

(8×7−4)×6 =312

Feedback

🎯Exact: you hit the target. You win!
🔥Close: within 10 points or 5% of the target, whichever is bigger.
🧊Far: further off. The badge shows how far, like “+42” (over) or “−17” (under).
